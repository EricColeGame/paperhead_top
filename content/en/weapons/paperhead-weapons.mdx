### Close-Range Weapons: Raw Power at a Cost

Close-range PAPERHEAD weapons hit incredibly hard, but they demand that you get uncomfortably close to enemies. This is a high-risk, high-reward style that punishes hesitation. Key traits of close-range weapons:

- Short effective range
- Often slow reload or cooldown times
- Excellent against single, large targets

### Mid-Range Weapons: The Backbone of Most Loadouts

Mid-range options are the most versatile in the PAPERHEAD weapons lineup. They don't excel in any single area, but they rarely let you down. For players still learning the game's enemy patterns, mid-range weapons offer a reliable foundation to build around.

### Long-Range Weapons: Patience Pays Off

Long-range weapons reward players who take the time to read enemy movement and position themselves smartly. According to player experience shared across community forums, long-range builds shine especially during multi-wave encounters where controlling space is critical.

## Tips for Upgrading and Unlocking PAPERHEAD Weapons

Progression in PAPERHEAD is tied closely to how you invest in your weapons. Rushing upgrades on the wrong weapon early can set you back significantly, so it pays to be strategic.

**Top upgrade priorities based on player experience:**

- **Focus on your primary weapon first.** Upgrading your main damage dealer provides the most immediate return on investment.
- **Don't neglect utility tools.** A well-upgraded crowd control option can save runs that would otherwise end in failure.
- **Unlock new weapon categories before maxing one out.** Having access to a diverse arsenal is often more valuable than a single maxed weapon.
- **Experiment in lower-stakes encounters.** Use early waves or easier stages to test new weapons before committing to them in high-pressure situations.

Unlocking new PAPERHEAD weapons typically involves completing specific challenges, reaching certain progression milestones, or discovering hidden paths within the game. The community has documented many of these unlock conditions, and resources like the [official PAPERHEAD page on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/) are great starting points for tracking down unlock requirements and patch notes.

## Advanced Combat Strategies Using PAPERHEAD Weapons

Once you've got a solid loadout and understand the upgrade system, it's time to think about how you actually use your PAPERHEAD weapons in combat. Mechanics matter, but execution is everything.

**Weapon swapping:** Don't stay locked into one weapon for an entire encounter. Fast weapon swaps let you exploit different damage windows and keep enemies off-balance.

**Ammo and resource management:** Running dry at the wrong moment is a run-ender. Always be aware of your resource levels and plan your aggressive pushes accordingly.

**Enemy targeting priority:** Not every enemy deserves your strongest weapon. Learn which enemies are the biggest threats and prioritize them with your most powerful tools, saving lighter weapons for weaker foes.

**Environmental awareness:** Many PAPERHEAD encounters have environmental features you can use to your advantage. Funneling enemies into choke points makes area-of-effect weapons dramatically more effective.
