| Pillar | Description | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|

| Resource Gathering | Collecting notes, keys, and light sources | Medium |
| Evasion | Actively avoiding the PAPERHEAD entity | Very High |
| Puzzle Solving | Decoding environmental clues to progress | Medium to High |

## Stealth and Detection: How the Entity Finds You

The detection system in the PAPERHEAD horror game is one of its most discussed mechanics among the community. Rather than relying on a simple line-of-sight or proximity trigger, PAPERHEAD's antagonist — the entity itself — responds to a layered combination of sound, light, and what many players describe as "behavioral tells."

### Sound-Based Detection

Sound is your biggest enemy. Every action you take generates noise, and the game's audio engine translates that noise into threat. Community reports suggest that even breathing patterns (managed through a stamina-adjacent mechanic) can influence detection thresholds in later chapters.

| Action | Noise Level | Detection Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Walking slowly | Minimal | Very Low |
| Running | High | Immediate |
| Knocking over objects | Moderate to High | Medium to High |
| Opening doors quickly | Moderate | Medium |
| Using items carelessly | Varies | Situational |

### Light and Shadow Mechanics

Light works as a double-edged sword. Carrying a light source helps you navigate the deliberately dark environments, but it also makes you visible. The entity is drawn to light in certain chapters, meaning players must decide when illumination is worth the risk.

- **Dim light sources** (candles, matches) offer minimal visibility but low detection risk
- **Bright light sources** (flashlights, lanterns) reveal more of the environment but attract attention
- **Ambient light** from environmental sources (windows, screens) creates safe zones where player-held light is less of a factor

## The PAPERHEAD Entity: Behavior Patterns and AI Design

The creature at the center of the PAPERHEAD horror game is not a mindless stalker. Player experience across the community consistently points to an antagonist that adapts, lingers, and learns within a single session. This pseudo-adaptive behavior is what elevates PAPERHEAD above simple chase-and-hide horror.

### Behavioral Stages of the Entity

Understanding the entity's behavioral phases is critical to long-term survival:

| Phase | Trigger | Entity Behavior | Player Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passive | No recent disturbances | Slow patrol, predictable path | Observe and plan movement |
| Alert | Heard a noise | Investigates area, heightened sensitivity | Freeze, control breathing |
| Pursuit | Spotted player or strong audio cue | Fast, direct movement toward player | Hide immediately, use distractions |
| Search | Lost sight of player | Methodical sweep of nearby area | Stay hidden, wait it out |
| Reset | Long period of quiet | Returns to passive patrol | Resume careful movement |

Community reports note that the entity does not always reset cleanly — sometimes it will "remember" a general area and return to it even after appearing to reset, punishing players who return to the same hiding spots repeatedly.

### What the Entity Reacts To

- **Repeated hiding spots** — using the same location twice in a session increases risk
- **Dropped items** — objects left in the path can either distract or alert, depending on timing
- **Player hesitation near doors** — lingering near thresholds appears to trigger alert states
- **Environmental changes** — if the player has altered the environment (moved objects, opened doors), the entity notices inconsistencies

## Puzzle Mechanics: Decoding a World Built on Paper

The PAPERHEAD horror game doesn't just ask you to survive — it asks you to *understand*. Puzzles are woven directly into the environment, requiring players to piece together fragmented documents, symbols, and spatial clues while under constant threat.

### Types of Puzzles in PAPERHEAD

| Puzzle Type | Description | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Document Assembly | Collecting torn pages and reassembling them for clues | Moderate |
| Symbol Matching | Identifying recurring symbols across different rooms | Moderate to Hard |
| Spatial Navigation | Using maps or environmental layouts to find hidden paths | Hard |
| Temporal Clues | Events that only make sense after experiencing multiple areas | Very Hard |
| Object Interaction | Using specific items in specific locations to unlock progress | Moderate |

What makes these puzzles particularly effective is that they never pause the threat. The entity continues its patrol while you're crouched over a torn document trying to make sense of faded handwriting. This design choice ensures that intellectual engagement and visceral fear exist simultaneously — a combination that defines the PAPERHEAD experience at its best.

### Tips for Solving Puzzles Efficiently

- **Take mental notes or screenshots** of symbols and text you can't immediately use
- **Prioritize document collection** in safe areas before attempting symbol puzzles
- **Listen for audio cues** — certain puzzle solutions are hinted at through ambient sounds
- **Don't rush** — incorrect interactions often generate noise that alerts the entity
- **Revisit areas after new discoveries** — earlier clues often become meaningful later
