## Puzzle Solutions and Strategies for Tough Sections

PAPERHEAD's puzzles range from intuitive to genuinely head-scratching. Here's a breakdown of general strategies that apply across the game's puzzle types, along with some specific tips for commonly frustrating sections.

### General Puzzle-Solving Framework

- **Think in layers.** PAPERHEAD almost always uses its paper metaphor literally. If a puzzle seems unsolvable, consider whether something needs to be placed underneath, on top of, or folded behind another element.
- **Revisit earlier rooms.** New abilities frequently unlock solutions to puzzles you encountered much earlier. Backtracking is almost always worth it.
- **Pay attention to color.** Color shifts in the environment are subtle but intentional. A slightly different shade on a wall or floor often indicates an interactive surface.
- **Don't skip NPC conversations.** Many NPCs drop puzzle hints disguised as casual dialogue. These are easy to dismiss as flavor text but frequently contain actionable clues.
- **Use the erase tool liberally.** Beyond its obvious puzzle applications, the erase tool interacts with far more of the environment than the game initially suggests.

### Commonly Missed Puzzle Mechanics

| Puzzle Type | Common Mistake | Correct Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Color-match puzzles | Matching exact shades | Match the *pattern*, not just the color |
| Weight puzzles | Using heaviest available object | Balance matters more than weight alone |
| Fold sequence puzzles | Folding in any order | Specific sequences trigger different results |
| Timing challenges | Rushing through | Deliberate, slow movement often bypasses hazards |
| NPC-based puzzles | Ignoring dialogue | NPC conversation order affects available solutions |
