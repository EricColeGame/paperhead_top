Many game soundtracks opt for high-fidelity production to showcase technical prowess. The PAPERHEAD soundtrack takes the opposite approach, and it's a deliberate creative choice. The lo-fi quality:

- Creates an impression of age and decay, matching the game's themes
- Makes the music feel diegetic — as if it exists within the world itself

This philosophy is closer to the work of composers like Akira Yamaoka (*Silent Hill*) or the ambient scores found in walking simulators and psychological horror games than to mainstream game music production.
