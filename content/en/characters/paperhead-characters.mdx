## Detailed Character Breakdowns

### Foldsworth — The Unyielding Shield

Foldsworth is the quintessential frontline fighter. Built from thick, layered cardboard, this character is designed to absorb punishment and keep enemies focused on him while allies reposition or deal damage.

**Key Strengths:**
- Highest base health in the roster
- Passive damage reduction from his "Layered Defense" ability
- Crowd-control tools that keep enemies pinned

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Slow movement speed
- Low damage output
- Struggles against fast, mobile opponents

|---|---|---|
| Health | 950 | 650 |
| Attack | 180 | 320 |
| Speed | 2.1 | 4.0 |
| Defense | 480 | 280 |

Foldsworth is ideal for players who want to learn the game's mechanics without worrying too much about dying. Community reports suggest that new players who start with Foldsworth tend to understand positioning and map awareness faster than those who jump into more complex characters.

### Crinkle — The Paper Blade

Crinkle is the glass cannon of the PAPERHEAD characters lineup. Sharp, fast, and deadly, Crinkle rewards players who can master timing and positioning. One wrong move, however, and Crinkle gets folded — literally.

**Key Strengths:**
- Highest single-target burst damage
- Evasion mechanics that make him hard to hit
- Excellent gap-closing ability called "Paper Dash"

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Lowest health pool in the game
- Requires precise execution to be effective
- Punished heavily by area-of-effect attacks

| Stat | Crinkle | Roster Average |
|---|---|---|
| Health | 320 | 650 |
| Attack | 580 | 320 |
| Speed | 6.8 | 4.0 |
| Defense | 120 | 280 |

Player experience from the community indicates that Crinkle has one of the highest skill ceilings in the game. Those who invest time into mastering his kit often describe the payoff as incredibly satisfying — but the learning curve is real.

### Papyra — The Healing Scroll

Papyra fills the support role with grace and utility. She's the backbone of any coordinated team, capable of keeping allies in the fight far longer than they'd survive alone. Her design draws from ancient scrolls and parchment, giving her an elegant, mystical appearance.

**Key Strengths:**
- Consistent healing output
- Buffs that amplify ally damage and speed
- A unique "Papyrus Shield" ability that absorbs a single lethal hit for a teammate

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Low personal damage
- Dependent on team coordination to shine
- Vulnerable when isolated

| Ability | Effect | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| Healing Fold | Restores 120 HP to target ally | 8 seconds |
| Ink Boost | Increases ally attack by 25% for 5s | 15 seconds |
| Papyrus Shield | Absorbs one fatal hit for ally | 45 seconds |
| Scroll Surge | AoE heal for 60 HP around Papyra | 20 seconds |

If you're playing in a group, having a Papyra on your team can dramatically shift the outcome of difficult encounters. Player experience suggests that teams running Papyra clear group content significantly faster than those without a dedicated support character.

### Tearaway — The Frantic Runner

Tearaway is the skirmisher built for players who love chaos. He darts in and out of combat, dealing moderate damage while staying almost impossible to pin down. His visual design — featuring torn edges and frayed corners — perfectly reflects his unpredictable nature.

**Key Strengths:**
- Exceptional mobility across all terrain
- Multiple dashes and escape tools
- Strong in one-on-one duels

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Moderate damage ceiling
- Requires constant movement to stay effective
- Can be overwhelmed by crowd control

Origami Rex is a fan-favorite among the PAPERHEAD characters for good reason. Folded in the shape of a dragon, Rex strikes a perfect balance between offense and defense, making him accessible to a wide range of players without sacrificing depth.

- Moderate survivability with a self-heal passive

### Laminate — The Trap Master

Laminate is the most unconventional of all the PAPERHEAD characters. Rather than fighting directly, Laminate specializes in laying traps, controlling zones, and punishing enemies who don't pay attention to the battlefield. His glossy, laminated surface makes him visually distinct — and deceptively dangerous.

**Key Strengths:**
- Unmatched area control
- Traps that deal damage and apply debuffs
- Excellent at holding chokepoints

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Requires significant setup time
- Weak in open, unstructured environments
- High skill floor — not beginner-friendly

## Tips for Mastering PAPERHEAD Characters

No matter which character you choose, these universal tips will help you get the most out of your experience:

- **Learn one character at a time.** Jumping between characters too quickly prevents you from developing deep mechanical knowledge of any single kit.
- **Watch how your character interacts with the environment.** PAPERHEAD's paper-themed world often has environmental interactions that vary by character.
- **Communicate in team modes.** Characters like Papyra and Laminate are dramatically more effective when your team knows what you're doing.
- **Don't neglect defensive options.** Even aggressive characters like Crinkle have defensive tools — learn them.
- **Experiment in low-stakes modes first.** Before taking a new character into competitive play, run them through easier content to build confidence.
